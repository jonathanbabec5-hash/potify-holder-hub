# POTIFY Greenhouse and Holder Hub marketing and sales plan

**Owner.** Chance owns the public release and approves campaign copy.

**Audience.** Community organizers and quest designers. **Offer.** Give members a place to record contributions and view non-transferable Seeds.

| Proposed timing | Public material | Desired outcome |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Days 1–3 | Walk through one fictional crew task from submission through approval and show the member's result. | Give the audience one verifiable reason to try the project. |
| Days 4–10 | Share the reviewed demo or sample through the project page and one relevant creator or community channel. | Try one demonstration quest. |
| Days 11–28 | Publish a build note showing what the trial revealed and one change made from feedback. | 5 completed sample quests. |

### Sales test

Trial an organizer support session for one free community season. Quote a fixed service price only when there is an organizer, scope and opt-in cohort. Test interest in an organizer-managed community season with a paid hosting or support fee.

### Measurement and decision

Use `holder-hub-first-public-build` for the pilot. Record a 28-day prelaunch baseline where available, then observe each participant for seven days after their first visit. Current audience, conversion and net revenue baselines are unknown. The numbers above are proposed targets. Distinguish visits, qualified actions, buyer inquiries and paid receipts. Keep private identifiers in the authorized reporting system. If the outcome cannot be attributed, label it unknown.

Proposed pilot budget is $0 for distribution and two founder hours for the first demonstration. At day 7, inspect the biggest point of friction. At day 28, expand only if the target action was measured and the sales test produced identifiable demand. Report fulfilled sales net of refunds and direct costs. A product serving trust or internal operations may have no direct sales at all.

**Prerequisite for external promotion.** This wallet-keyed beta predates the multi-wallet ID pilot. Existing member data and production migration remain separate. Draft copy and placements go through the existing approval process.
